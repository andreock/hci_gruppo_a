About the project

The problem

In neighbourhoods where families have fewer resources, middle school students do few science experiments. After-school clubs often lack the materials, and educators have not had the chance to learn how to use them.

For many of these students, science remains something to read about in a book, not something to try with their own hands.

What the group did

The Scienza di quartiere group brought weekly workshops to six after-school clubs run by associations in Barriera di Milano and Falchera. It prepared kits with simple experiments, trained the educators and provided refurbished tablets.

The project came from the associations, which proposed it and built it together with the University: it is an example of a partnership with outside organisations, as explained on the «Propose a project» page.

The results

In all, 140 students took part, with 32 workshops in six after-school clubs. Kits and materials stayed with the associations, which continue the workshops with their own funds.
