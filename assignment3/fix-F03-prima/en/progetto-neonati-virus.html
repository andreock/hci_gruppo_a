About the project

The problem

Every winter many newborns are admitted to hospital with respiratory viruses such as RSV (respiratory syncytial virus), which can make breathing difficult in the first months of life. Babies born prematurely are the most vulnerable.

A newborn does not leave home alone: the virus arrives through the people close to the baby. But little is known about who brings it into the family and which everyday precautions really help.

What the group will do

The NeoVir group will follow 300 volunteer families for one winter. When someone at home has symptoms, the family takes a simple swab with the kit they received, without having to go to hospital.

Molecular tests will show which viruses are going around. Statistical analysis will then link the results to daily life, to find out who brings viruses into the family and which precautions work.

What it will leave behind

Each family will receive its own results. Together with family paediatricians, the group will write a practical guide for parents and present it at meetings open to everyone.
