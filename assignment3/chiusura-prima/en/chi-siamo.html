Our story

We started with a simple question: what happens to good ideas that are too small for a major grant, but too useful to stay in a drawer?

  1. 2021 A question over coffee

    During a break in the department, a researcher mentions giving up on a study because she was a few thousand euros short of the sensors she needed. Others around the table have similar stories. Over the following weeks that conversation becomes a list of stalled projects and a proposal to the University: ask people directly for help.

  2. 2022 A name and a rule

    The ideaBlume Office is born: three people and a shared desk. The name joins «idea» with «Blume», German for flower, because an idea only blooms if someone looks after it. That same year we write the rule we still follow, «all or nothing»: if the goal is not reached, donations go back to the donors.

  3. 2023 The first campaign

    With «Social robots for older people's independence», the site and the first edition of the University programme start together: if a project reaches its goal, the University adds an amount equal to the goal. The group passes its target and we see that the idea works.

  4. 2024 The neighbourhood, and the first missed goal

    With the after-school science workshops, associations from Barriera di Milano and Falchera build a project together with the University: the start of our partnerships with local organisations. That same year, the risk map for mountain huts misses its goal. We return the donations and say so clearly on the project page: that too is part of our promise to donors.

  5. 2025 More topics, more people

    Very different projects reach the site, from maths formulas readable with a screen reader to bacteria that break down microplastics. Alongside first-time donors, more people follow every update and come back to support a new project.

  6. 2026 A site designed for everyone

    We redesign the site starting from donors and researchers: clearer text, accessible pages, a complete English version and the same numbers on every page.

  7. Today A story still being written

    We have published 10 projects. 1499 supporters have donated €54,155 and the University has added €27,000. Applications for the 2027 edition of the programme run from 1 November 2026 to 31 March 2027.

What we do

We stand between the labs and the people who want to support them. Our work starts before a campaign goes online and ends only when the group has accounted for its spending.

  • We choose the projects

    We publish only projects by research groups at the University of Turin. Before publication we review each page with the group, so that anyone can understand the project without knowing the subject.

  • We check the numbers

    We check each group's budget and rewards before publication: every expense must be clear and realistic, and together they must add up exactly to the campaign goal.

  • We run the match funding

    We run the University co-funding programme. We support the selected projects during the campaign and, if they reach their goal, the University adds the same amount, up to €10,000 per project.

  • We report back

    We ask every group for updates during the work and a final report with the actual spending. We publish it on the project page and send it to the donors, so everyone can see what their contribution achieved.

Where the money goes

If the goal is reached, the money goes to the group's department, earmarked for the project: it cannot be used for anything else. Every project page shows how the funds are used, item by item, and, once the work is finished, the report with the actual spending.

If the goal is not reached, donations go back to the donors. Only the payment fee, which you always see before confirming, is not returned. You can find all the details in the FAQ.

The people in the Office

ideaBlume is run by the University's ideaBlume Office. From the original three people we have grown into a team of seven with different skills: former researchers who help groups prepare their campaigns, people who look after accounts and reports, people who work on the text and people who answer the phone and email. If you write to us, we reply within 2 working days.
