About the project

Many young people say they feel worried about the climate. Yet we know little about how heat, news and social media change their mood day by day.

The EcoMente group will develop a simple, anonymous diary app: for three months, 400 high-school and university students will answer two questions a day. We will compare the answers with weather data.

With the results we will prepare materials for teachers and student support services.
