How ideaBlume works

ideaBlume collects donations for research projects at the University of Turin. Here are the steps for donors, for people proposing a project and for groups with an ongoing campaign.

On this page

  1. For people supporting a project
  2. For people proposing a project
  3. For groups with an ongoing campaign
  4. How much it costs
  5. Still have questions?

For people supporting a project

  1. Choose a project

    Look at the topic, the group, the use of funds and the status of the campaign.

    See ongoing projects

  2. Choose a reward or a free donation

    Free donations start at €5. Rewards are small thank-you gifts from the group.

    What is a reward

  3. Enter your details and choose whether to show your name

    No need to register. You can donate anonymously.

    Who sees my name

  4. Pay securely

    By card, PayPal, Satispay or bank transfer. Before confirming you see the total including the fee.

    Payment methods and fees

  5. When the campaign ends we tell you the result

    Goal reached: the money goes to the group and, for programme projects, the University matches it. Goal not reached: we return your donation.

    How the match works

For people proposing a project

  1. Check who can propose

    Research groups at the University of Turin; associations, schools and organisations can partner with a group.

    Who can propose a project

  2. Read the programme requirements and deadlines

    Goal, use of funds, rewards and call dates.

    Programme requirements

  3. Send your application

    A form of about 10 minutes. Your data stays saved while you fill it in.

    Go to the application form

  4. Take part in training and selection

    The ideaBlume Office will contact you with the dates.

    Programme phases

  5. Prepare your page with the Office

    Texts in Italian and English, rewards, budget.

    Guidance for groups

  6. Share the results

    Publish updates and, at the end, a report with the spending.

    How to publish updates

For groups with an ongoing campaign

  • Updates: send text and photos to the Office (programma@ideablume.it); we publish them within 2 working days. [TO BE CONFIRMED with the University]
  • Content in two languages: write each text once in Italian and once in English, in two separate fields: the site shows one language at a time. Title up to 80 characters, subtitle up to 160.
  • Goal exceeded: ask the Office to add a second goal with an amount and a purpose: it will appear in the box next to “Support”.
  • A problem with the site? Report it with the contact form, choosing “Problem with the site”.

How much it costs

For donors: ideaBlume adds no extra cost to your donation. The payment provider charges a fee (for example 1.8% + €0.35 by card, PayPal or Satispay), shown before you confirm. [TO BE CONFIRMED with the University]

For groups selected by the programme: any publication fee and included services [TO BE CONFIRMED with the University]. This prototype shows no amounts because the sources do not report them.

For associations and external organisations: proposing a partnership is free and does not commit your organisation.
