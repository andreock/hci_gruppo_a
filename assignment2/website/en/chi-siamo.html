About us

ideaBlume is the crowdfunding site for research projects at the University of Turin. It is run by the University's ideaBlume Office. [TO BE CONFIRMED with the University]

What we do

  • We publish only projects by research groups at the University of Turin.
  • We check each group's budget and rewards before publication.
  • We run the University co-funding programme.
  • We ask every group for a final report with the spending, published on the project page.

Where the money goes

If the goal is reached, the money goes to the group's department, earmarked for the project. If not, it goes back to the donors. [TO BE CONFIRMED with the University]

This site is a prototype

ideaBlume is a prototype created by students of the Human-Computer Interaction course at the University of Turin. Projects, people, contacts and amounts are fictional; information still to be confirmed is marked with [TO BE CONFIRMED with the University].
