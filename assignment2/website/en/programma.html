The University co-funding programme

In one sentence: every year the University of Turin selects some projects from its research groups, supports them during the campaign and, if they reach their goal, adds an amount equal to the goal, up to €10,000 per project. [TO BE CONFIRMED with the University]

The programme described here is simulated and inspired by real university crowdfunding initiatives.

Phases of the edition

  1. Training completed

    Sessions to learn how to present the project and run a campaign.

  2. Selection completed

    A University committee chooses projects based on quality, impact and feasibility.

  3. Support completed

    The ideaBlume Office helps groups prepare their page, rewards and budget.

  4. Fundraising and match in progress

    Campaigns last 30 to 60 days. When the goal is reached the University adds the same amount.

How the match works

  • The match applies only if donations reach the goal by the end date.
  • The University adds an amount equal to the goal, up to €10,000 per project. [TO BE CONFIRMED with the University]
  • Donations above the goal go to the project's second goal, if there is one, and are not matched. [TO BE CONFIRMED with the University]
  • If the goal is not reached, donations are returned and the University adds nothing (“all or nothing”).

An example

The “Healthy hives” project has a goal of €6,000. Supporters have donated €7,800: the goal is reached, so the University adds €6,000. The extra €1,800 goes to the second goal. In total the project receives €13,800.

Who can apply

Research groups at the University of Turin led by a permanent staff member (professor or researcher). Research fellows and PhD students can be part of the group. [TO BE CONFIRMED with the University]

Project requirements

  • A financial goal between €3,000 and €10,000. [TO BE CONFIRMED with the University]
  • A use of funds explained item by item.
  • Results that the public can understand and a plan to share them with supporters.
  • Simple rewards that the group can really deliver.

How and when to apply

Fill in the application form before the call closes. Dates for the 2027 edition: [TO BE CONFIRMED with the University]

How much it costs

For donors: ideaBlume adds no extra cost to your donation. The payment provider charges a fee (for example 1.8% + €0.35 by card, PayPal or Satispay), shown before you confirm. [TO BE CONFIRMED with the University]

For groups selected by the programme: any publication fee and included services [TO BE CONFIRMED with the University]. This prototype shows no amounts because the sources do not report them.

For associations and external organisations: proposing a partnership is free and does not commit your organisation.
