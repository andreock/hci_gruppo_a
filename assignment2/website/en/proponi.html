Propose a project

There are two ways to bring a project to ideaBlume: apply to the University programme, if you are part of a research group at the University of Turin, or propose a partnership, if you represent an association, a school or another organisation.

How much it costs

For donors: ideaBlume adds no extra cost to your donation. The payment provider charges a fee (for example 1.8% + €0.35 by card, PayPal or Satispay), shown before you confirm. [TO BE CONFIRMED with the University]

For groups selected by the programme: any publication fee and included services [TO BE CONFIRMED with the University]. This prototype shows no amounts because the sources do not report them.

For associations and external organisations: proposing a partnership is free and does not commit your organisation.

Who can propose

  • Do you do research at the University of Turin?

    You can apply to the co-funding programme with your group's project. You need a financial goal, a clear use of funds and a permanent staff member as principal investigator.

    Apply to the programmeRequirements and deadlines

  • Do you represent an association, a school or an organisation?

    You cannot launch a campaign on your own, but you can propose an idea to carry out with a research group: the ideaBlume Office looks for the right group and puts you in touch. [TO BE CONFIRMED with the University]

    Propose a partnership

Timing

  • Applications to the programme: once a year, dates [TO BE CONFIRMED with the University]
  • Reply to a partnership proposal: within 10 working days, by phone or email. [TO BE CONFIRMED with the University]
  • Campaign length: 30 to 60 days.

Examples of projects with the local community
