About the project

Every winter many newborns are admitted to hospital with respiratory viruses such as RSV. Babies born prematurely are the most vulnerable.

The NeoVir group will follow 300 volunteer families for one winter: with a simple swab at home we will learn which viruses are going around, who brings them into the family and which precautions really work.

Families will receive the results and a practical guide written together with family paediatricians.
