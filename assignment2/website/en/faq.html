For people supporting a project

Donations and payments

Can I donate without choosing a reward?

Yes. Choose “Free donation” and enter the amount, from €5 upwards.

Rewards are just a thank-you: they are not required.

Link to this answer

What is the minimum amount?

€5 for a free donation, for every project.

For a reward, the minimum is the price shown next to it.

Link to this answer

Do I need to register to donate?

No. We only need your name, surname and email for the confirmation.

Link to this answer

How can I pay and how much does it cost?

You can pay by: Credit, debit or prepaid card, PayPal, Satispay, Bank transfer.

The payment provider charges a fee, which you see in the summary before confirming (for example 1.8% + €0.35 by card, PayPal or Satispay). There is no fee from us for bank transfers. [TO BE CONFIRMED with the University]

Link to this answer

My payment did not go through: what should I do?

Nothing has been charged. You can try again straight away: the details you entered stay saved.

Try another payment method, for example a bank transfer.

If the problem continues, call us on 011 019 4250 (Monday to Friday, 9:30–13:00 and 14:00–17:00 (Italian time)): we will help you complete the donation.

Link to this answer

Campaign rules

What happens if the project does not reach its goal?

We return your donation: this is the all-or-nothing rule.

The refund goes back to the same payment method within 15 days of the end date. The payment fee may not be refunded. [TO BE CONFIRMED with the University]

Link to this answer

The project has already reached its goal: is it still worth donating?

Yes, as long as the campaign is open. Extra donations go to the second goal shown in the project box.

Donations above the goal are not matched by the University. [TO BE CONFIRMED with the University]

Link to this answer

How does the University match work?

If a programme project reaches its goal, the University adds an amount equal to the goal, up to €10,000. [TO BE CONFIRMED with the University]

Read an example.

Receipt, privacy and rewards

Can I donate anonymously? Who sees my name?

Yes: in the form choose “Donate anonymously”. The public sees “Anonymous supporter”.

The research group still receives your name and email to thank you and send your reward. The amount is never public. [TO BE CONFIRMED with the University]

Link to this answer

Will I get a receipt? Is the donation tax-deductible?

After donating you receive a confirmation email with a summary.

Tax receipt and tax relief: [TO BE CONFIRMED with the University]

Link to this answer

When will I get my reward?

After the campaign closes, if the goal is reached. The group will write to ask for your address, if needed.

For people proposing a project

Taking part

Who can propose a project?

Research groups at the University of Turin, with a permanent staff member as principal investigator. [TO BE CONFIRMED with the University]

Read who can propose.

Link to this answer

We are an association or a school: can we take part?

Yes, as a partner of a research group: propose a partnership. The ideaBlume Office looks for the right group. [TO BE CONFIRMED with the University]

Sending the proposal does not commit you and is free.

Link to this answer

When can we apply?

Once a year, through a call. Dates of the next edition: [TO BE CONFIRMED with the University]

Link to this answer

How much does it cost to propose a project?

Proposing a partnership is free.

For groups selected by the programme, any publication and service costs: [TO BE CONFIRMED with the University]

Link to this answer

During the campaign

How long does a campaign last?

30 to 60 days.

Link to this answer

How do we publish updates and the final report?

Send text and photos to the Office (programma@ideablume.it): we publish them in the project's “Updates” section. [TO BE CONFIRMED with the University]

At the end we ask for a report with the spending, which we also send to supporters.

Link to this answer

Do we have to write content in two languages?

Yes, in Italian and in English, in separate fields: the site shows one language at a time. Read the guidance.

General questions

ideaBlume

Who runs ideaBlume?

The University of Turin's ideaBlume Office. [TO BE CONFIRMED with the University] About us.

How do I know the money really goes to research?

Every project publishes the use of funds item by item and, at the end, a report with the actual spending.

The money goes to the group's department, earmarked for the project. [TO BE CONFIRMED with the University]
