About the project

When an infection is serious, doctors must choose an antibiotic before knowing which one will work. Current tests take one or two days.

The RapidAB group is developing a small chip where the patient's bacteria grow in contact with several antibiotics. A fluorescent light shows within hours which antibiotics stop them.

Using the right antibiotic straight away helps patients and reduces antibiotic resistance.
