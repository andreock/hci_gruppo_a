About the project

Official monitoring stations are few and far from schools. Yet that is where children spend much of their day.

The Aria Aperta group will build twenty low-cost sensors with ten school classes, using open software and designs, and install them near school entrances. The data will be on a public map.

All materials will be available in Italian and English so that other cities can replicate the project.
