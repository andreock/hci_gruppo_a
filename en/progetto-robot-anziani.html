About the project

The problem

Many older people want to stay in their own home for as long as possible. Sometimes, though, small difficulties such as forgetting a medicine are enough to put this choice in doubt and worry those close to them.

Many technologies designed to help them are too complicated for the very people who need them most.

What the group did

The CareBot group programmed a tabletop robot that is easy to use, by voice and with large buttons. The robot gives medicine reminders, suggests memory exercises and alerts relatives when help is needed.

The group tested it with 24 people in three care homes and at home, and prepared materials to help families use it.

The results

18 out of 24 people said the robot helped them remember their medicines.

The software is open, so anyone can use and improve it, and a Turin company is already adapting it.
