Phases of the edition

  1. Training completed

    Sessions to learn how to present the project and run a campaign.

  2. Selection completed

    A University committee chooses projects based on quality, impact and feasibility.

  3. Support completed

    The ideaBlume Office helps groups prepare their page, rewards and budget.

  4. Fundraising and match in progress

    Campaigns last 30 to 60 days. When the goal is reached the University adds the same amount.

How the match works

  • The match applies only if donations reach the goal by the end date.
  • The University adds an amount equal to the goal, up to €10,000 per project.
  • Donations above the goal go to the project's second goal, if there is one, and are not matched.
  • If the goal is not reached, donations are returned and the University adds nothing (“all or nothing”).

An example

The “Healthy hives” project has a goal of €6,000. Supporters have donated €7,800: the goal is reached, so the University adds €6,000. The extra €1,800 goes entirely to the organisation, without being matched. In total the project receives €13,800.

Who can apply

Research groups at the University of Turin led by a permanent staff member (professor or researcher). Research fellows and PhD students can be part of the group.

Project requirements

  • A financial goal between €3,000 and €10,000.
  • A use of funds explained item by item.
  • Results that the public can understand and a plan to share them with supporters.
  • Simple rewards that the group can really deliver.

How and when to apply

Fill in the application form before the call closes. Dates for the 2027 edition: from 1 November 2026 to 31 March 2027.
