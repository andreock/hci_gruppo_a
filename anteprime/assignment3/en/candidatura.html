Apply to the University programme

Before you start

  • You need: project title, goal in euros, use of funds, names of the group.
  • Time needed: 10 minutes.
  • Your data stays saved in this browser tab until you send it or close the tab.
  • Call deadline: 31 March 2027.

There is 1 field to correct

    All fields are required, except those marked as optional.

    Who is applying
    Your University address, for example name.surname@unito.it.
    The project
    Up to 80 characters, in plain words.
    Between €3,000 and €10,000.
    What you want to do and why it is useful. Up to 600 characters.
    The main items with amounts.
    Names and roles, one person per line.
