About the project

The problem

As glaciers retreat, some rock faces become unstable. Popular mountain huts and trails can end up exposed to landslides and rockfalls that were rare until a few years ago.

People who run a hut or choose a hike have little information about which slopes are moving.

What the group wanted to do

The GeoAlp group wanted to survey ten slopes near mountain huts with a laser scanner and install displacement sensors, which can measure even small movements of the rock.

With this data it would have published an interactive risk map for hikers and hut managers.

What happened

The campaign stopped at 62% of the goal and donations were returned. The project was not part of the co-funding programme.

The group thanked its supporters and announced that it will propose the project again.
