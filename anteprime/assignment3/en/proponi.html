Who can propose

  • Do you do research at the University of Turin?

    You can apply to the co-funding programme with your group's project. You need a financial goal, a clear use of funds and a permanent staff member as principal investigator.

    Apply to the programme

  • Do you represent an association, a school or an organisation?

    You cannot launch a campaign on your own, but you can propose an idea to carry out with a research group: the ideaBlume Office looks for the right group and puts you in touch.

    Propose a partnership

If you do research: from idea to campaign

Applications to the University programme open once a year. Here is what happens, step by step.

  1. Check the requirements

    You need a financial goal, a use of funds explained item by item, rewards the group can really deliver and a permanent staff member as principal investigator.

    Read the programme requirements

  2. Send your application

    The form takes about 10 minutes and your data stays saved while you fill it in. For the 2027 edition, applications run from 1 November 2026 to 31 March 2027.

    Go to the application form

  3. Take part in the training and selection

    At the meetings you learn how to explain your project and run a campaign. A University committee then chooses the projects based on quality, impact and feasibility.

    The programme stages

  4. Prepare the page with the Office

    The ideaBlume Office helps you with text, rewards and budget. Every text must be written in Italian and in English.

  5. Launch the campaign

    Fundraising lasts 30 to 60 days. If you reach the goal, the University adds the same amount, up to €10,000.

    How the match works

If you represent an organisation: from idea to partnership

Local associations, schools, organisations and businesses can start a project together with the University. Sending a proposal is free and does not commit you to anything.

  1. Tell us your idea

    Fill in the form, explaining which problem you would like to tackle and who would benefit. You do not need scientific expertise.

    Go to the partnership form

  2. Get a reply

    Within 10 working days someone from the ideaBlume Office contacts you, by phone or email.

  3. Meet the research group

    The Office looks for a University of Turin group interested in the idea and puts you in touch. Together you decide whether and how to take it forward.

  4. Build the project together

    The research group leads the project and the organisation takes part as a partner. It has already happened with the after-school science workshops, started by associations in Barriera di Milano and Falchera.

    Read the after-school project

What makes a strong proposal

Donors usually do not know the subject and want to understand quickly why the project matters. These four points help with both the application and the campaign.

  • A clear problem

    Explain in a few sentences which problem you are tackling and who it matters to. If someone who does not know the subject understands it, you are on the right track.

  • A realistic goal

    Ask for the amount you really need and explain every expense: together they must add up exactly to the campaign goal.

  • Results to share

    Decide from the start what you will be able to show donors, even while the work is under way: data, photos, small milestones.

  • Simple rewards

    A postcard or a lab visit is enough, as long as the group can really deliver it. Anyone who prefers can always make a free donation.

Timing and costs

  • Applications to the programme: once a year. For the 2027 edition: from 1 November 2026 to 31 March 2027.
  • Reply to a partnership proposal: within 10 working days, by phone or email.
  • Campaign length: 30 to 60 days.
  • Costs: proposing a partnership is free and groups selected by the programme pay no fees. Read how much it costs.

For groups with an ongoing campaign

Is your campaign online? Here is how to keep it alive and who to turn to when you need help. For any question you can write to the Office: programma@ideablume.it.

  • Publish updates

    Send text and photos to the Office: we publish them within 2 working days in the project's “Updates” section. Even a small step forward helps your supporters feel part of the work.

  • Write in two languages

    Write each text once in Italian and once in English, in two separate fields: the site shows one language at a time. The title can be up to 80 characters, the subtitle up to 160.

  • Reply to supporters

    Messages sent with “Write to the group” come straight to you. We tell people who write that you usually reply within 2 working days.

  • If you exceed the goal

    Ask the Office to add a second goal with an amount and a purpose: it will appear in the box next to “Support”. Donations above the goal are not matched by the University.

  • When fundraising closes

    If the goal is reached, write to people who chose a reward to ask for their address, if needed. When the work is finished, send the report with the actual spending: we publish it on the project page and send it to supporters.

  • If something does not work

    For a mistake on the project page or another problem with the site, write to us with the contact form, choosing “Problem with the site”.

Still have questions?

You can find more answers in the questions for people proposing a project. For the programme you can write to programma@ideablume.it, for partnerships to collaborazioni@ideablume.it.
