Propose a partnership with a research group

For local associations, schools, organisations and companies. Tell us your idea: the ideaBlume Office looks for an interested research group at the University of Turin and puts you in touch.

Sending this form does not commit your organisation and is free.

    Your organisation

    What you would like to do, with whom and for whom. A few lines are enough.
