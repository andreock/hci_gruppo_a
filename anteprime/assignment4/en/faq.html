For people supporting a project

Donations and payments

Can I donate without choosing a reward?

Yes. Choose “Free donation” and enter the amount, from €5 upwards.

Rewards are just a thank-you: they are not required.

What is the minimum amount?

€5 for a free donation, for every project.

For a reward, the minimum is the price shown next to it.

Do I need to register to donate?

No. We only need your name, surname and email for the confirmation.

How can I pay and how much does it cost?

You can pay by: Credit, debit or prepaid card, PayPal, Satispay, Bank transfer.

The payment provider charges a fee, which you see in the summary before confirming (1.8% + €0.35 by card, PayPal or Satispay). There is no fee from us for bank transfers.

My payment did not go through: what should I do?

Nothing has been charged. You can try again straight away: the details you entered stay saved.

Try another payment method, for example a bank transfer.

If the problem continues, call us on 011 019 4250 (Monday to Friday, 9:30–13:00 and 14:00–17:00 (Italian time)): we will help you complete the donation.

Campaign rules

What happens if the project does not reach its goal?

We return your donation: this is the all-or-nothing rule.

The refund goes back to the same payment method within 15 days of the end date. The payment fee is not refunded.

The project has already reached its goal: is it still worth donating?

Yes, as long as the campaign is open. Extra donations go to the second goal shown in the project box.

Donations above the goal are not matched by the University.

How does the University match work?

If a programme project reaches its goal, the University adds an amount equal to the goal, up to €10,000. This is also called co-funding.

Read an example.

Receipt, privacy and rewards

Can I donate anonymously? Who sees my name?

Yes: in the form choose “Donate anonymously”. The public sees “Anonymous supporter”.

The research group still receives your name and email to thank you and send your reward. The amount is never public.

Will I get a receipt? Is the donation tax-deductible?

Yes: after donating you receive the summary and the tax receipt by email.

Donations to the University are tax-deductible: keep the receipt for your tax return.

When will I get my reward?

After the campaign closes, if the goal is reached. The group will write to ask for your address, if needed.

For people proposing a project

Taking part

Who can propose a project?

Research groups at the University of Turin, with a permanent staff member as principal investigator.

Read who can propose.

We are an association or a school: can we take part?

Yes, as a partner of a research group: propose a partnership. The ideaBlume Office looks for the right group.

Sending the proposal does not commit you and is free.

When can we apply?

Once a year, through a call. For the 2027 edition: from 1 November 2026 to 31 March 2027.

How much does it cost to propose a project?

Proposing a partnership is free.

Groups selected by the programme pay nothing either: training, support and publication are paid for by the University.

During the campaign

How long does a campaign last?

30 to 60 days.

How do we publish updates and the final report?

Send text and photos to the Office (programma@ideablume.it): we publish them in the project's “Updates” section.

At the end we ask for a report with the spending, which we also send to supporters.

Do we have to write content in two languages?

Yes, in Italian and in English, in separate fields: the site shows one language at a time. Read the guidance.

General questions

ideaBlume

Who runs ideaBlume?

The University of Turin's ideaBlume Office. About us.

How do I know the money really goes to research?

Every project publishes the use of funds item by item and, at the end, a report with the actual spending.

The money goes to the group's department, earmarked for the project.

How can I talk to a person?

Call us on 011 019 4250 (Monday to Friday, 9:30–13:00 and 14:00–17:00 (Italian time)) or write to aiuto@ideablume.it.

ideaBlume words

The fundraising

Crowdfunding

Raising money online: many people donate small amounts to make a project happen.

Goal

The minimum amount the project must raise by the end date. If it is not reached, donations are returned.

All or nothing

The ideaBlume rule: the group receives the money only if the goal is reached by the end date. Otherwise we return your donation.

Second goal

A higher amount, with a specific purpose, for projects that have already reached their goal. Donations above the goal go to it.

Supporter

Someone who donates to a project.

Research group

The University people who propose and carry out the project. The principal investigator leads the group.

The donation

Reward

A small thank-you from the group for people who donate at least a certain amount, for example a postcard or a lab visit. It is not required.

Free donation

A donation without a reward, of the amount you prefer, from €5 upwards.

Fee

The cost charged by the payment provider (card, PayPal, Satispay). You see it before confirming your donation.

The programme and the results

University programme

The initiative through which the University selects some projects, supports them and co-funds them.

Year (edition)

The year in which the campaign took place. For programme projects it is the programme edition.

Final report

The text in which the group describes the results and how it spent the money.
