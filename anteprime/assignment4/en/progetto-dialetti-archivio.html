About the project

The problem

In many villages in Piedmont, only older people still speak the local dialect. Words, sayings and stories that nobody has ever written down risk disappearing with them.

Every year there are fewer voices to listen to, and what is not recorded today cannot be recovered.

What the group wanted to do

The Voci del Piemonte group wanted to record 200 interviews in 40 villages and publish them in a free online archive, with transcripts. That way anyone could have listened to these dialects, from scholars to families.

Meetings in the villages were also planned, to find people to interview and to share the work with them.

What happened

The campaign stopped at 62% of the goal and, under the all-or-nothing rule, donations were returned.

The work did not stop completely: with a small departmental fund the group recorded 35 interviews, which can be found in the library archive.
